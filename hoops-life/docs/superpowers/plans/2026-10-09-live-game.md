# Live Game Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** Use superpowers:executing-plans inline; one fresh final reviewer for this milestone. Steps use checkbox syntax.

**Goal:** Play a regular-season game through actual possession decisions and persistent consequences.
**Architecture:** `HL.createGame` exposes a generator around the existing possession loop; `simGame` drains it. `HL.LiveGame` journals commands/seed/snapshots and commits through League; `LiveGameUI` presents the scoreboard and choices.
**Tech Stack:** Existing JavaScript/CSS, Node and Playwright.
**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-09-live-game-design.md`

## Global Constraints
- Preserve exact seeded baseline simulation for unattended games, real headshots and existing modes.
- No arbitrary ability/minutes controls for Career; no fake result/news/salary before final commitment.
- Regular-season first; clearly state pending postseason, substitutions, timeouts and challenges.
- JSON/IndexedDB persistence, reduced motion, mobile width and validated choices.

## Review Focus
- Reconstruction must not consume the live league RNG or change past results.
- Reload/finish twice cannot duplicate salary, stats, standings or media.
- Active games prevent roster/rule/tendency edits and competing simulation.
- A bench/DNP Career player can choose intentions without forcing their way onto the floor.
- Invalid/stale commands cannot mutate the command journal or saved league.
