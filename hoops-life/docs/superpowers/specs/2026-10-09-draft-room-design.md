# Playable draft room

Build the next queued Franchise milestone: scouting, saved lottery results, draft-night decisions and rookie consequences. The user has authorized continued implementation without repeated approval stops. Preserve existing real headshots, smaller team jerseys and era presentation.

The league's current draft runs invisibly inside season rollover. Add a JSON-owned `draftRoom` with the upcoming year, prospect objects, private scouting reports, shortlist, lottery, pick slots and selections. Preparation is available during the season; the lottery and picks require offseason. Reopening or reloading cannot generate another class, reroll lottery results or draft a player twice. Team ID zero is valid. Inspection consumes no RNG. GM/owner choose for their team; coach can inspect and delegate.

Historical classes use the next season's real NBA rookies and their historical draft positions as a consensus signal; future/random classes use generated prospects. This first version shares the existing database's limitation: historical draftees who have not yet appeared in NBA season rows are not rated prospects. Do not fabricate exact pre-draft scouting statistics, combine measurements or historical pick ownership. Picks belong to their original teams in this sandbox; trades/swaps/protections and expansion-draft rights are subsequent work.

Lottery rules: reverse record with seeded tie-breaks; 2019+ draws four with published 14-team weights; 1994–2018 draws three with the weighted table (normalized for smaller fields); 1990–1993 uses descending weights; 1987–1989 draws three equally; 1985–1986 draws the entire non-playoff order equally. Before 1985, 1966–1984 uses a coin flip between the last-place clubs in each conference then reverse record; earlier drafts use reverse record, with territorial selections not modeled. Publish these exact limits. Round two uses reverse record, independently of lottery; pre-1989 three rounds is the existing model's simplified cap.

Each club gets 12 private scouting sessions per class. Workout and interview can each be performed once on a prospect. Reports have uncertain ability/ceiling ranges and dated notes; workouts narrow uncertainty and interviews expose personality ranges. Report/shortlist inspection is read-only and does not disclose exact attributes or future accolades.

The UI has a lottery stage with descending reveal cards and an optional reduced-motion/skip path; reveal progress and results persist. Draft night has an on-the-clock club, prospect cards, private reports, shortlist, selection confirmation and a pick ticker. Sim to own pick pauses; delegate finishes the remaining draft. Selecting locks the slot and prospect immediately. A prepared incomplete room blocks season rollover until picks finish, while existing headless/legacy advancement can still auto-draft if no room was prepared. Commit the class exactly once, preserving chosen rookie contracts/team destinations through rollover and automatic trimming.

Announcements quote the selected player, owner and fans. Own picks generate relationship memory and a five-team-game watch; actual minutes, points, games played and results generate follow-up coverage, including honest DNPs. Private workouts/interviews stay out of public news. Readiness must handle trades/retirement canceling the watch, save/reload, roster crowding, missing next-year data and coach permissions.

Out of this milestone: Player Career, Live Game, staff, pick trading, full CBA/rookie-scale negotiations and full historical draft rights. These stay visible in the roadmap.
