# Gameplay depth

User authority: continue actual gameplay development with many meaningful options, reactive people and media; no direct controller action or ratings cheats. Franchise coaches can choose arbitrary eligible five-man lineups, return control to automatic rotations, spend timeouts, call named plays with participants, assign defensive matchups, and address the team in their own words with an explicit communicative intent. Career players control all eleven personal tendencies and call a play involving themselves while on court; they cannot force substitutions or timeouts.

Decisions must affect the existing possession engine, preserve save/replay and exact-once completion, remain atomic when invalid, and never change unattended calibration. Calls expire on the next own-team possession. Participants who have left the floor invalidate a call rather than teleporting into play. Manual rotations persist, but injured/fouled-out players leave and are replaced. Timeout recovery is bounded by legal inventory; modern last-three-minute and fourth-quarter limits apply. Historical full-timeout inventory excludes the separate old short-timeout category, explicitly disclosed. No replay/challenge or postseason claim in this milestone.

Postgame coverage cites actual participants, play outcomes, substitutions, fatigue and statistics. Player/coach responses depend on performance, treatment and personality; memory changes happen once at completion. Custom huddle words are preserved, with response intent explicitly chosen rather than pretending arbitrary language interpretation. Repeated huddles cannot stack performance bonuses.
