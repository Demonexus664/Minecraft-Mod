# Media freedom and credibility overhaul

The user explicitly rejected the restrictive four-topic composer, repetitive fixed quotes and generic reactions. Success means authoring personal words, selecting a subject and outlet, keeping conversations going, and seeing responses depend on actual people, relationships, events and source evidence. Continue autonomously under the user's existing authorization.

Extend PlayerPosts rather than create a competing media subsystem. Preserve legacy saves and public cards. Add grouped intents (ordinary statement, support/work, praise, callout, apology, recruitment, trade request, role demand, coaching criticism, rule criticism, point/win promises, celebration, private message, selective excerpt, fabricated quote, full context). Custom words remain exact and escaped; intent explicitly supplies meaning instead of pretending a text parser understands arbitrary sentences. Named targets can be any person in the current save. Career speaks only as its player; Franchise authors its own roster. Other players retain their own responses.

Remove hard posting and daily reply limits. All valid choices produce a conversation; per-person/per-day consequence caps prevent reputation farming without suppressing actions. Keep replied messages as a thread and allow typed responses. Public/private visibility is explicit. Private messages reach the target, not the news feed; choosing to publish an excerpt can expose one's own recorded message. Source snapshots retain full words, exact contiguous excerpt, original identity/team/date and record IDs. Fabrications carry an unverified label and produce a later evidence-based correction, not an asserted authentic interview.

Audience voices choose situation-specific branches from personality, personal trust, team relationship, recent actual game, saved previous statements, and the exact topic/words. Record why a person responded and remember the interaction. Vary phrasing without consuming league simulation RNG. Avoid having every ordinary remark become breaking news or every player issue a public clapback. NPC posts use multiple natural captions; stats live in the source facts rather than every caption. Historical channels and language remain appropriate.

Point/win promises resolve only against a recorded scheduled fixture; DNP, departure and removed fixtures cannot become invented achievements. Coverage compares the exact pledge with actual results. Recruitment, criticism and apologies affect remembered trust, morale/fans and Career reputation/coach trust within bounded budgets, without force-signings or ability boosts. Broader accepted trade chains, investigations and arbitrary prose interpretation remain future work and are not claimed implemented.

Verification: ownership/atomic rejection; repeated valid posting/replies with capped rewards; private leakage/source fidelity; custom text/targets; distinct relationship/context responses; actual promise/DNP/fabrication correction; save replay and RNG preservation. Browser-test real photos, typed statements, target/outlet/source selection, ongoing replies, save/load, Career faceless identity and mobile. One fresh final review after implementation.
