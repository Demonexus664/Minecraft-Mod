# Interviews, misleading excerpts and consequences

The user wants realistic media that responds to their freedom, including interviews cut out of context. Build recorded full statements, edited excerpts with visible source context, responses and delayed assessments in both Career and Franchise. These are authored fictional press scenes within this save, supported by real player photographs; this milestone does not download or falsely relabel real-world interview footage.

Keep an immutable statement record (speaker, current team, season/day, full words, tone and game facts). On the first statement and then every third eligible statement, if no current thread is open for that speaker, publish a misleading excerpt using actual words from that statement. Modern presentation is a quote/social clip; old eras use an excerpt in print/radio. Never publish private family/meeting material as an interview.

Career postgame tones record the exact chosen answer. Franchise gets a Press Room with current-team players and authored tone choices; no global historical team overwrite. An excerpt has a full-statement view and one response: publish context, wait for games, or confront the framing. Context publication partially repairs fan confidence/trust; confrontation costs trust. Responses are persisted, atomic and cannot be farmed. Do not make every voice agree.

Assess the next three actual team games, counting appearances/DNPs and real points/minutes/wins. Quote context and outcomes separately; good play does not prove a caption true or false. Retirement or a team departure cancels that pending assessment rather than fabricating a verdict. Store source, response and memories across save/reload, and expose them alongside current media.

Tests must cover exact full quote/excerpt provenance, private/public boundaries, response ownership and idempotency, era format, actual-game near misses/DNPs, departure cancellation and JSON/IndexedDB. Preserve all prior playable modes and seeded simulation behavior.
