# DNA refinement: preserve the published game

Base: `0c35ce9` on `hoops-overhaul-2026-10`. Existing historical ratings, legendary pulls, skill categories, stories, tactical calls, real photos, season film and interactive postseason remain.

Chemistry is an immediate relationship. Mutations require verified card seasons, relevant inherited categories, elite tools and a compatible physical build. Three arbitrary famous names never qualify. Authored transformations have a family, priority and replacement relationship; at most two nonconflicting families may transform a build. Evolved recipes replace their precursor. Missing evidence fails closed. Qualification consumes no simulation randomness.

Abilities carry ingredient evidence, conditions and basketball explanations. Player signatures transfer only through relevant skill categories. Team chemistry requires its partners on the floor; private signatures never spread to unrelated teammates. Reapplying DNA is idempotent. Pure basketball context functions expose gravity, screens, movement, post doubles, contact balance, passing lanes, rotations and rebounding position. The possession simulator consumes these and records bounded action counters for verification and inspection.

Legendary fusion uses ingredient portraits/cards, category-specific choreography, a staged transformation and the resulting ability. Ordinary chemistry stays in the panel. Escape/skip always work, queued reveals clean up timers and focus, reduced motion goes directly to a readable result, and failed portraits retain names and attributes. The five normal Skill Draft cards stay visible independently of animations; a rare wildcard remains a bonus sixth card.

Ordinary prime duration means a basketball prime, not an unconditionally decades-long plateau. Physical decline begins before skill decline, injuries compound attrition, and demand considers effectiveness and availability. Only a jointly extraordinary longevity/prime/durability/stamina/IQ build gets the extreme curve. Automatic play stops after sustained lack of NBA demand; manual grinding and possible return remain available. No fixed retirement age.

82-0 clutch choices must resolve a genuine remaining possession through the existing game generator. Do not append fabricated points/overtime to a finished game. Scores, boxes, quarters and series winners must agree. Existing four-round challenge format remains a fast alternate-history challenge rather than full franchise management.

Acceptance: contextual short/tall transformations; historical and fictional chemistry; unrelated categories do not inherit signatures; duplicate/evolved bounds; mutation frequency samples; actual action differences and same-seed baseline preservation without DNA; meaningful longevity distributions; 82-game and postseason score accounting; real-browser five-card visibility, repeated spins, category fusion, escape, reduced motion, mobile and failed assets; regression suites.
