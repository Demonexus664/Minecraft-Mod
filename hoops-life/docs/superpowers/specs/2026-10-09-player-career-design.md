# Player Career: first playable NBA loop

The full MyCareer/BitLife vision remains much larger than one milestone. This continuation must replace the disabled title-screen mode with a coherent NBA-prospect-to-retirement loop, preserving detailed creation and the reactive world. No rating editor or forced roster moves. The user requested continued development loops; implementation proceeds under that authorization.

Create a player with name, hometown, nationality, age 19–22, position, primary/secondary archetypes, height 72–90 inches, weight 160–340 lb, wingspan from height to height+10, dominant hand, number, personality and a college/international route. Body choices affect strengths and mobility; finite prospect ability and growth caps prevent a free 99. A preview uses an isolated RNG snapshot and changes neither the live league nor save. The created player has a faceless name/number card. Insert into any supported real season; a simulated draft placement derives from prospect quality and team need, with earned rotation minutes. In this milestone, creation starts at NBA entry; playable school/college, replacing a real player, PEDs, detailed legal/health/business systems and post-career jobs remain queued.

`L.career` owns identity, player ID, coach/agent/family relationships, energy/happiness/fame/reputation, money, weekly action allowance, training progress, timeline, press scenes, promises, game log and season reports. One shared league sim powers all games and playoffs. Tendencies edit only this player and must influence the same possession engine. User cannot select rosters/ratings/contracts for others.

Organized actions: Basketball (skill training, film study, recovery, role discussion, teammate support, trade request), Life (family time, charity, nightlife, buy a car), Media (postgame interviews and endorsement offer). Each has clear time/money costs and effects; three decisions per calendar week prevent spam. Private family/training stays private; visible choices generate era-appropriate reactions. Partying creates low energy until a next-game assessment, which reports actual performance; bragging predicts a 20-point next-game outcome and is judged from the actual box score, not a scripted success. Teammate support tracks a named player's like/respect/trust. Cash checks are strict and no action creates accidental debt. Salary arrives pro rata across scheduled team games, with simplified tax/agent withholding. No duplicate game income or repeated outcome scenes after reload.

Energy changes only the career player's bounded performance multiplier, with rest between days; default sim remains identical for non-career players. Development is incremental training credit plus actual-game use, capped per attribute and earned over time. Coach trust and current roster competition govern minutes; role requests cannot override coach decisions. Career rookies must displace minutes fairly, not inherit the full real roster's 240-minute squeeze. Offseason snapshots retain stats, awards, record and playoff series; expiring contracts offer plausible team options with their salaries and roster strength. Under contract, requests may be refused; accepted moves affect roster, relationships and actual role. Retirement and return to the league are reversible choices with age/decline and available contracts.

A dedicated career hub separates Today, Basketball, People, Life, Media and Story. Show a focused next-game stage, faceless identity, meters, earned role, actual last-game report, pending scenes and a chronological storyline. No unrelated Franchise editor controls. Load/save dispatch preserves career mode. Simple quick simulation remains usable, while choosing each next game supports detailed decisions. Browser tests cover creating the player, actions, tendencies, games, a factual presser response, finances, save/reload and mobile width. Tests cover invalid creation/actions, seed preservation, no paid/instant progression, real simulation effects, exact-once income/results, role competition, offseason deals, retirement/unretirement and legacy Franchise saves.
